Click the game to start. Arrows move the cursor, Space places a toy, hold Space and press left/right to choose a toy, Enter is Game Reset. P pauses, M mutes.

Toy Wars is an original Atari 2600 game in progress: a 16K cartridge with Atari's Super Chip, running here on a 6502 and TIA emulator. Defend the toy box: place toys on the shelves with batteries, and stop the monsters before they reach it. Each shelf's lid slams once; the second monster through ends the game. Source and design.
